# Milestone 5 — Learning & Analytics

Status: COMPLETE

Milestone 5 extends AI Studio without changing the AI Core, workspace, source,
conversation, or RAG contracts.

## Delivered

- Flashcards with difficulty/category metadata, shuffle, reveal, navigation,
  and JSON/Markdown export.
- Mixed-format Quizzes with 5/10/20 questions, three difficulty levels,
  auto-grading, score, answer review, explanations, and regeneration.
- Study Guides with Overview, Important Concepts, Definitions, Examples,
  Recommended Reading, and Learning Path.
- Deterministic CSV/XLSX analysis: summary, profiles, missing values, duplicates,
  correlations, distributions, IQR outliers, statistics, trends, and chart
  suggestions.
- Bar, line, pie, scatter, histogram, and area charts implemented with Recharts,
  responsive rendering, and PNG/SVG/CSV/JSON/Markdown export.
- Additive AIOutput enum values and migration; no unrelated schema redesign.
- Accessible responsive viewers within the existing Workspace shell.

## Boundaries

Marketplace, Teams, Billing, AI Agents, OCR, Podcast, Audio Overview, Knowledge
Graph, and Plugins are not implemented. Work stops after Milestone 5 pending
approval for any later milestone.
